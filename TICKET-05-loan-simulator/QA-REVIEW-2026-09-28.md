### 🟠 F-19: Division by zero at 0% interest is unhandled at the formula level

**Where:** `useLoanMath.ts` — the standard amortization formula divides by
`(1 - (1+r)^-n)` where `r` is the periodic rate; at `r = 0` this is `0/0`.

**Reproduction:** Call the calculation function directly with `interestRate: 0` → `NaN`.

**Current mitigation:** The UI never lets this happen — `isFormValid` requires
`interestRate > 0`. That avoids a crash, but it also **silently rejects a legitimate loan
input** (0% interest is a real product, e.g. promotional financing), and nothing documents
that this was a deliberate trade-off rather than an oversight.

**Fix direction:** Either handle `rate === 0` explicitly in the formula (monthly payment =
`principal / months`), or, if 0% is genuinely out of scope for this exercise, say so in
`TICKET.md`'s scope/out-of-scope section instead of enforcing it silently in a validator with
no comment.

---

### 🟡 F-20: Rounding loses cents

**Where:** `useLoanMath.ts` uses `Math.round()` on the final payment figure.

**Observation:** Both reference cases in the last review's table are correct to the nearest
dollar but lose the cents (`856.0748` → `856`, not `856.07`). For a financial calculation,
`.toFixed(2)` (formatted as currency, not rounded to an integer) is the expected precision.
